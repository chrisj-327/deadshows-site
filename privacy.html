Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Dead Shows does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your favorites, recently played shows, playback position, downloaded recordings, and app settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see this information and it is never transmitted to us.

Content and network requests

Concert recordings, artwork, and show information are streamed and downloaded directly from the Internet Archive (archive.org). When you browse or play a show, your device requests that content from the Internet Archive, whose own terms and privacy policy govern those requests.

No analytics or tracking

Dead Shows contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Children

Dead Shows does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
