Dead Shows

The fastest way to hear the Grateful Dead's live legacy.

Thousands of Grateful Dead concerts spanning 1965 to 1995, streaming free — every recording drawn from the Internet Archive's Grateful Dead collection, the same tapes traded by Deadheads for decades. Dead Shows just makes them easy to find and beautiful to browse.

What you can do

  • Browse every year and every show, or jump to "Today in Grateful Dead History."
  • Search by date, venue, city, song, or guest appearance.
  • Expand any show to read its full setlist — and compare setlists side by side.
  • Pick your recording: soundboard, matrix, or audience — or let Dead Shows auto-select the best-rated version.
  • Gapless playback, background audio, lock-screen controls, and offline downloads.
  • Favorite shows and individual songs, and pick up right where you left off.
